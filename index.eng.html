Brown Crab

To catch brown crabs, all you need is a flashlight and quick reflexes—the last thing a brown crab sees is a flash and a snapping hand. You must act fast: within a second or two, the crab realizes what's happening and uses its claws to pinch you with all its strength, and it won't let go easily. Catching crabs not only provides delicious food but also gives an adrenaline rush.
